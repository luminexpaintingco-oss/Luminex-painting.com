Questions

Before your estimate.

What types of painting does Luminex focus on?

Residential interior painting, including walls, ceilings, trim, baseboards, interior doors and frames.

Do you paint cabinets?

Cabinet painting is not part of our current service focus. Luminex is currently focused on interior walls, ceilings, trim, baseboards, doors and frames.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. You can book a free 30-minute on-site estimate online, submit the estimate request form, or call Luminex to get started.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the project details you provide and contact you to discuss the scope and next steps for the estimate.

What areas do you serve?

Concord and Kannapolis, NC are our primary service areas. We also consider painting projects in Charlotte, Harrisburg, Salisbury, Huntersville, Mooresville, Davidson and Cornelius, depending on project scope and scheduling.

Does the estimate include preparation?

The project scope can include preparation such as addressing small holes, sanding where needed and other surface prep based on the condition of the areas being painted.
