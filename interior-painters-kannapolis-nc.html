What affects an interior painting estimate?

The size of the rooms is only one factor. Wall condition, ceiling height, the amount of patching or sanding needed, trim and door work, color changes and the finish selected can all affect the scope. That is why Luminex bases estimates on the actual surfaces and preparation required rather than promising a one-size-fits-all price online.

A preparation-first process.

Before finish coats are applied, the project is reviewed for the preparation it needs. Depending on the condition of the space, that can include protecting adjacent surfaces, addressing small holes or rough areas, sanding where appropriate and preparing surfaces for a cleaner final finish. The completed work is then reviewed during the final walkthrough.
